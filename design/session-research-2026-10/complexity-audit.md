# The Dark Council — Complexity Audit

The load-bearing classification found no Decorative mechanic. This audit therefore
identifies where to reduce **teaching and memory cost without removing rules**.

Scores are estimates before live instrumentation:

- Teach burden: 1 (seconds) to 5 (several minutes).
- Table reach: 1 (one role) to 5 (everyone).
- GM load: 1 (none) to 5 (continuous private tracking).
- Moments: **Unknown** until the reaction map is used; predictions are not evidence.

## Initial-teach budget

The full rulebook should not be taught. The common initial explanation can fit in
**six minutes** by teaching only:

1. premise and victory;
2. three-round loop;
3. private negotiation and transfer window;
4. Council's Expose/Interrogation options;
5. death does not remove participation;
6. five/two Evidence budget;
7. personal cards carry exact Goal/Secret text.

Teach later:

- Auction at the start of Round 2;
- detailed death procedure on first death;
- Candidate/Reveal/Election procedure immediately before the Final;
- all Syndicate operations privately.

## Complexity conclusion

The largest burden is not one expendable mechanic. It is **simultaneous delivery of
four interlocked layers**: Goals, Secrets, Evidence, and Council resolution. Cutting
one breaks several others. Layered teaching externalises the burden without changing
the game.

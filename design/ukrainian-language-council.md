# Ukrainian Language Council decision

**Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Scope:** `Темна Рада_latest_ua.md`, Ukrainian playtest aids, and Ukrainian web UI.

The text was reviewed independently by four perspectives:

1. modern Ukrainian tabletop-game localizer;
2. political-thriller and noir literary editor;
3. normative Ukrainian linguist and syntactician;
4. live-table GM and player-UX critic.

## Adopted terminology

| English | Ukrainian | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Blackmail (Secret) | **Шантаж** | Keep. |
| Extortionist (Ambition; formerly Blackmailer) | **Вимагач** | Renamed to prevent confusion with the Secret «Шантаж». |
| Intrigue (Goal-table category; formerly Secrets) | **Інтриги** | Renamed to avoid overlap with the `Секрет` mechanic. |
| Expose a Secret | **Викриття Секрету** | Always use the complete term in headings and quick references. |
| Faction Reveal | **Розкриття Фракції** | Always use the complete term to distinguish it from Викриття. |
| named player outside your Faction | **ім'я гравця з іншої Фракції** | Replaces the calque «відомий чужинець». |

The Council retained **Реформіст, Криївка, Замовлення, Компромат, Відмивання,
Підвищення**. The majority judged these established terms clear and central to the
game's Ukrainian political-noir voice.

## Style rules

- Use modern spoken Ukrainian for teaching and quick-reference text.
- Keep the rulebook precise, but replace administrative calques where a shorter
  Ukrainian construction carries the same mechanic.
- Use `1 Вплив` in nominative/accusative constructions and `2–10 Впливу`;
  prepositions may require `1 Впливу`.
- Use `порога` consistently.
- Address an individual reader as `Ви`; address the room in the teaching script as
  plural `ви`.
- Preserve exact game-term capitalization, but do not capitalize ordinary prose for
  atmosphere alone.

## Mechanical wording clarified during review

- Round 2 Private Phase is 45 minutes in every document.
- An Expose target must be another living player.
- The Interrogation initiator needs at least 1 Influence remaining after payment to
  vote.
- Innocent-facing rules now explain succession and the true lead after executing the
  Assassin.
- Death moves positive Influence only.
- Full Syndicate wipeout divides Stash equally, rounded down; remainder returns to
  Bank.
- A dead Accomplice cannot be promoted.
- Candidate and Hit edge cases now have explicit fallbacks.

These edits change terminology and adjudication clarity, not the selected balance
package.

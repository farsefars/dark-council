# The Dark Council — Player Reference

## Track only five things

1. **Influence:** your chips plus any debt recorded by the GM.
2. **Faction:** Aristocrat, Reformer, or Magnate.
3. **Goals:** your Motive (+5) and Ambition (+10), claim by end of Round 2.
4. **Secret:** can be Exposed once.
5. **Information:** your Advantage, outsider clue, deals, and suspicions.

## Game flow

- **Round 1:** Hit → Private 30 min → Council → Top 3.
- **Round 2:** living players +2 → Hit → Auction → Private 20 min → Council → Top 3.
- **Round 3:** living players +2 → Hit → Private 15 min → Council → Final.

## Private Phase

- Move around, talk, lie, bargain, and transfer Influence.
- Influence may be transferred **only now**.
- Deals are not binding.
- The GM gives warnings with 5 minutes and 1 minute remaining.
- An ally may transfer Influence to clear your debt. Received Influence repays debt
  first.

## Ability to Pay and Bankruptcy

- Exposing is allowed only with at least 2 Influence.
- A living player may vote in an Interrogation only with at least 1 Influence. Ghosts
  vote regardless of balance.
- A negative balance before the Final is debt, not Bankruptcy.
- After the Round 3 Council, the GM declares every player still below zero Bankrupt.
  Only that status removes personal Faction Victory eligibility.

## Council

1. GM resolves assassination and Hit.
2. Ghosts ask one shared private Yes/No question.
3. Open discussion, Expose, and Interrogations.

### Expose

Name a player and Secret. Correct: target -4, you +1. Wrong: you -2.
A correctly Exposed Secret cannot be scored again.

### Interrogation

Accuse the current Assassin. Cost starts at 4 and rises by 1 each time.
Strict majority; tie fails.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Vote fails | Accused +3 |
| Innocent Executed | Each living Guilty voter -1 |
| Assassin Executed | Initiator gets cost back and +5; each living Guilty voter +5 |

After any Execution, the GM says only whether the player was current Assassin, and
all Evidence holders read their exact original statements.

## Death and Ghosts

- Half your positive Influence, rounded down, goes to Stash if Assassinated or Bank
  if Executed; give the rest to one living player.
- You still vote in Interrogations and the Final and join the Ghost question.
- You cannot hold, receive, spend, transfer, or initiate an Interrogation.

## Final

1. Three richest living players nominate three different living, non-Bankrupt Candidates.
2. Everyone gets one private Reveal vote; living players may buy extras for 1.
3. Everyone gets one secret Election vote; living players may buy extras for 3.
4. Vote spending is removed before Magnate/Syndicate totals are checked.

## Victory

- **Aristocrat/Reformer:** your Faction's Candidate wins.
- **Magnate:** living Magnates finish with the required combined Influence.
- **Declared Bankrupt or Syndicate:** you do not personally receive a Faction Victory;
  the rest of that Faction remains eligible to win.

> The Syndicate reference is optional for Innocents.

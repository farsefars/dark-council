# The Dark Council

Latest English rules. Designed for 13-15 players; supports 8-15.

## 0. The Game in 60 Seconds

You are members of a secret council competing for Influence before a final election.
During three rounds, you will trade, bargain, expose Secrets, pursue personal Goals,
and decide whether accused players should be Executed.

Two players secretly form the **Syndicate**: the **Assassin** and the
**Accomplice**. The Assassin kills once per round. The Syndicate is always made up of
one Aristocrat and one Reformer. Magnates are never Syndicate members.

### How you win

- **Aristocrat:** an Aristocrat Candidate wins the Final Election.
- **Reformer:** a Reformer Candidate wins the Final Election.
- **Magnate:** the Magnates finish with enough combined Influence.
- **Syndicate:** the current Assassin survives and finishes with enough personal
  Influence plus Influence in the Syndicate Stash.

Bankruptcy fixed at the start of the Final and Syndicate membership **make a player
personally ineligible for a Faction Victory**. The rest of that player's Faction
remains eligible to win.

If the real Assassin is Executed, everyone who voted Guilty gains +5 Influence, and
the GM announces a true lead about the surviving Syndicate member.

### What should I read?

- **Innocent player:** read Sections 0-10. Section 11 is optional.
- **Assassin or Accomplice:** read the entire document, including Section 11.
- **Game Master (GM):** also read Section 12.

In this document, **must** means required, **may** means optional, and **cannot /
must not** means prohibited. A consequence stated without "may" always applies.

---

## 1. Game Structure

The game has five parts:

1. **Preparation**
2. **Round 1**
3. **Round 2**
4. **Round 3**
5. **The Final**

### Round 1

Hit -> Private Phase (30 minutes) -> Council -> Leaderboard

### Round 2

Each living player receives a **+2 Influence stipend**.

Hit -> Auction -> Private Phase (30 minutes) -> Council -> Leaderboard

### Round 3

Each living player receives a **+2 Influence stipend**.

Hit -> Private Phase (30 minutes) -> Council -> The Final

At the start of every round, the GM publicly announces a Hit: an assassination
order for the Syndicate. It names a trait of the victim the Syndicate must kill, never
a specific player.

---

## 2. Preparation

The GM meets each player privately.

Each player receives:

- **4 starting Influence**
- A **Faction:** Aristocrat, Reformer, or Magnate
- A **Role:** Innocent, Assassin, or Accomplice
- A **Motive:** a small personal Goal worth +5 Influence
- An **Ambition:** a large personal Goal worth +10 Influence
- A **Secret:** Blackmail, Treason, Corruption, or Heresy
- The name of one player who is **not** in their Faction; their exact Faction is not
  revealed
- One **Starting Advantage**

The Starting Advantage is one of the following:

- **Evidence:** one statement about the Syndicate. It may be false.
- **Secret Intelligence:** the truthful Secret of one named player.

The Assassin and Accomplice learn each other's identities. No one else learns a Role.

The following information is public:

- The number of Aristocrats, Reformers, and Magnates
- The Syndicate contains exactly one Aristocrat and one Reformer
- Magnates are never members of the Syndicate
- There are five pieces of Evidence, exactly two of which are false

---

## 3. The Round

### 3.1 Hit

The GM publicly reads the round's Hit: an assassination order for the Syndicate.
It names a trait of the victim, such as "a Magnate" or "a player in the current Top
3." It never names a specific player.

### 3.2 Private Phase

During the Private Phase, players are allowed to move throughout the house, speak
privately, make deals, share or invent information, and transfer Influence.

Influence may be transferred only during the Private Phase. All agreements are
non-binding.

▶ During this phase, the Assassin submits one assassination target to the GM.

### 3.3 Council

Everyone gathers in the Council area.

Resolve the Council in this order:

1. The GM announces the assassination result and whether the Hit succeeded.
2. Ghosts ask their question.
3. The floor opens for accusations, negotiation, Exposing Secrets, and
   Interrogations.
4. The GM closes the Council.

### 3.4 Leaderboard

At the end of Rounds 1 and 2, the GM publicly announces the names and current
Influence totals of the Top 3 living players.

---

## 4. The Economy

- **Currency:** Influence. One chip equals one Influence.
- **Transfers:** players may physically transfer Influence during the Private Phase
  only.
- **Payments:** Influence paid as a cost goes to the Bank unless a rule says
  otherwise.
- **Ghosts:** cannot hold, receive, spend, or transfer Influence. Any future income is
  discarded.

### Ability to pay

A living player may voluntarily take an action only if their personal Influence can
cover the **largest immediate penalty** that action could cause. Borrowing Influence
or promising to pay later is not allowed.

In particular:

- attempting to Expose a Secret requires at least 2 Influence; and
- participating in an Interrogation vote requires at least 1 Influence.

Ghosts are exempt: they vote but never pay or receive Influence. An involuntary loss
that the player did not choose still applies in full and may create debt.

### Bankruptcy

If an involuntary loss exceeds a player's personal Influence, the GM records the
negative balance as **debt**. Any Influence that player later receives immediately
repays the debt; the player receives only the remainder after the debt is cleared.

During a Private Phase, another living player may transfer Influence to a player in
debt. The transferred Influence immediately repays that debt. Allies can therefore
clear a debt during the Round 3 Private Phase before the Final begins.

**Debt before the Final is not yet Bankruptcy and does not itself remove victory
eligibility.**

Immediately after the Round 3 Council and before Candidate nominations, the GM checks
every balance once. A player below zero at that moment is **Bankrupt** for the entire
Final. That player:

- cannot be nominated as a Candidate; and
- does not personally receive a Faction Victory, even if their Faction wins.

The rest of the Faction remains eligible to win. A Bankrupt player still participates
in the Reveal and Final Election and retains one free vote in each. Bankruptcy status
does not change after the Final begins.

## 5. Your Goals

Every player receives one Motive and one Ambition. They are assigned independently;
the columns below are only a quick reference, not fixed pairs.

| Reward | Greed | Justice | Secrets | Alliances |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **+5 Motive** | Vanity | Wrath | Espionage | Commerce |
| **+10 Ambition** | Collector | Radical | Blackmailer | Diplomat |

### Motives: claim by the end of Round 2

- **Vanity:** finish Round 1 or Round 2 on the Leaderboard.
- **Wrath:** initiate an Interrogation, regardless of its result.
- **Espionage:** privately name to the GM both the **Motive and the Ambition** of one
  other player, correctly. Nobody is told anyone else's Goals at setup, so this can
  only be done by talking to people.
- **Commerce:** give Influence to or receive Influence from three different players
  during Private Phases.

### Ambitions: claim by the end of Round 2

- **Collector:** win the Auction.
- **Radical:** vote Guilty on two different players who are then Executed.
- **Blackmailer:** extortion — receive at least **2 Influence** from each of two
  different players over the course of the game.
- **Diplomat:** form a Public Pact with two players who belong to the other two
  Factions. All three players stand before the GM and publicly declare their Factions.
  The Pact succeeds only if all three declarations are truthful.

Tell the GM as soon as you complete a Goal. The GM pays its reward after verifying it.

## 6. The Council

### 6.1 Expose a Secret

**Cost:** free

Only a living player who has at least 2 Influence before declaring the guess may
attempt to Expose a Secret.

Publicly name a player and guess their Secret.

- **Correct:** the target loses 4 Influence; you gain 1 Influence.
- **Incorrect:** you lose 2 Influence.

**Each Secret can be correctly Exposed only once.** It then becomes public knowledge
and is no longer a valid Expose target.

Any Influence lost but not awarded to another player goes to the Bank. The GM confirms
only whether the guess was correct.

### 6.2 Interrogation

An Interrogation accuses one player of being the current Assassin.

**Cost:** the first Interrogation in the game costs 4 Influence. Every later
Interrogation costs 1 more than the previous one.

1. The initiator names the accused and pays the current cost.
2. The accused may make a final statement.
3. Every Ghost and every living player who has at least 1 Influence when voting begins
   votes publicly. A living player with less does not cast a vote. This rule also
   applies to the accused.
4. A strict majority of all votes cast is required for Execution. A tie fails.

| Result | Consequence |
|---|---|
| The vote fails | The accused survives and gains +3 Influence. The initiator's cost is not refunded. |
| The vote passes; the accused is not the Assassin | The accused is Executed. The initiator's cost is not refunded and there is no reward. Everyone who voted Guilty loses **1 Influence**. |
| The vote passes; the accused is the Assassin | The accused is Executed. The initiator's cost is refunded and the initiator gains +5 Influence. Everyone who voted Guilty gains **+5 Influence**. |

Ghosts neither gain nor lose Influence for their vote.

After an Execution, the GM publicly states only whether the executed player was the
current Assassin.

Then resolve the mandatory Evidence disclosure in Section 7.

### 6.3 Auction

The Auction happens at the start of Round 2, after the stipend and Hit but before
the Private Phase.

The prize is one piece of **guaranteed truthful Evidence**.

1. The GM opens bidding at 1 Influence.
2. Living players make public, whole-number bids.
3. Each new bid must be higher than the previous bid.
4. The highest bidder pays the winning bid to the Bank.
5. The GM gives the Evidence privately to the winner.

If no one bids, the GM repeats the Auction after the Round 2 Private Phase with an
opening bid of 0.

---

## 7. Evidence

There are exactly **five** pieces of Evidence:

- Four are distributed as Starting Advantages.
- One is reserved for the Round 2 Auction.
- Exactly **two of the five are false**.
- The Auction Evidence is always truthful.

Evidence refers to the **Syndicate**, not only to the original Assassin. A truthful
statement may describe either the Assassin or the Accomplice.

The five Evidence templates are:

1. "One Syndicate member's Motive is **[Motive]**."
2. "One Syndicate member's Ambition is **[Ambition]**."
3. "One Syndicate member's Secret is **[Secret]**."
4. "The Assassin, not the Accomplice, is the **[Aristocrat/Reformer]**."
5. "One Syndicate member's Starting Advantage was **[Evidence/Secret
   Intelligence]**."

You may repeat, conceal, or lie about Evidence during normal play. The GM never
confirms a statement merely because someone says it.

After any Execution, every Evidence holder must publicly read their original statement
word for word.

---

## 8. Death and Ghosts

When a player is Assassinated or Executed:

1. Resolve half of their Influence, rounded down:
   - if Assassinated, move it to the Syndicate Stash;
   - if Executed, return it to the Bank.
2. The victim immediately gives all remaining Influence to one living player of their
   choice.
3. The victim becomes a Ghost.

A Ghost:

- keeps eligibility for a Faction Victory unless another rule makes them ineligible;
- retains one vote in every Interrogation and in the Final Election;
- cannot initiate an Interrogation;
- cannot hold, receive, spend, or transfer Influence.

At the start of each Council, all Ghosts have 60 seconds to agree on one Yes/No
question for the GM. **The GM answers truthfully to the Ghosts only, in private.** The
Ghosts may then tell the living whatever they like — including a lie. The GM may reject
compound questions or questions that cannot be answered Yes or No; the Ghosts may
immediately rephrase.

---

## 9. The Final

The Final begins after the Round 3 Council. Before any other Final action, the GM
fixes Bankruptcy under Section 4.

### 9.1 Candidates

The three living players with the most Influence each nominate one living,
non-Bankrupt player as a Candidate. They may nominate themselves.

Each nomination must create a different Candidate. If players are tied for a
nominating position, all players make a single public vote among the tied contenders to
decide who receives the nomination right. The GM resolves another tie randomly.

### 9.2 Reveal

1. Every player, including Ghosts, privately gives the GM the name of one player whose
   Faction they want revealed. It may be a Candidate or anyone else.
2. Each player has one free Reveal vote.
3. Living players may publicly buy additional Reveal votes for 1 Influence each.
4. The GM reveals the Faction of the player with the most Reveal votes.

If there is a tie, reveal every tied player's Faction.

### 9.3 Buy Votes

Every player, including each Ghost and each Bankrupt player, has one free Final
Election vote.

Living players may buy extra votes for **3 Influence per vote**. Influence spent on
votes is removed before victory totals are checked.

### 9.4 Secret Election

Every player submits a secret ballot naming one Candidate and the number of votes they
are casting. The Candidate with the most votes wins. A tie is resolved by a second
secret ballot between the tied Candidates; purchased votes remain valid.

### 9.5 Victory Check
The GM resolves victory in this order:

1. Count the current Assassin's personal Influence plus the Syndicate Stash.
2. Count the combined remaining Influence of all Magnates.
3. Identify the winning Candidate's Faction.
4. Apply personal ineligibility from Bankruptcy or Syndicate membership.

Multiple groups may win the same game.

---

## 10. Victory Conditions

Use the thresholds for the current player count in Section 12.

### Aristocrats

You win if an Aristocrat Candidate wins the Final Election.

### Reformers

You win if a Reformer Candidate wins the Final Election.

### Magnates

You win if the combined remaining Influence of all Magnates reaches the required
threshold after Final Election spending. The winning Candidate's Faction does not
matter.

### Personal ineligibility

You do not personally receive a Faction Victory if either of the following is true:

- the GM declared you Bankrupt at the start of the Final; or
- you are the Assassin or Accomplice.

Your Faction remains eligible to win. If it wins, every other member who has not lost
personal victory eligibility also wins.

### Syndicate

The Assassin and Accomplice win together if the current Assassin:

- is alive at the Victory Check; and
- has at least the required combined total of personal Influence plus the Syndicate
  Stash.

# 11. ▶ The Syndicate

> **Syndicate section — optional reading.**
>
> Everything you need to remember is already above: the Syndicate is an Assassin and
> an Accomplice, and the Assassin kills once per round.

## 11.1 Membership and cover Factions

The Syndicate is always:

- one Aristocrat; and
- one Reformer.

The GM announces this structure during Preparation. Magnates are never Syndicate
members.

Your Faction is real for all public rules: you count in its announced size, may claim
it truthfully, and may vote for its Candidate. However, Syndicate membership removes
your personal Faction Victory eligibility. Your shared objective is the Syndicate
Victory.

## 11.2 The Syndicate Stash

The Stash begins at 0 and is tracked privately by the GM.

Influence in the Stash:

- belongs to the Syndicate, not to either player;
- cannot be stolen, and cannot be used to buy votes at the Final;
- counts toward the Syndicate Victory;
- may be withdrawn by the current Assassin, up to 2 per round, to spend;
- **passes intact to the promoted Accomplice** if the Assassin is Executed; and
- is confiscated and split among the living **only if both Syndicate members are dead**.

## 11.3 Hits

At the start of every round, the GM publicly reads one Hit. A Hit describes
the required victim by a trait, never by name.

- **Success:** if the assassination victim meets the Hit, add 3 Influence from
  the Bank to the Stash.
- **Failure:** remove 3 Influence from the Stash. If the Stash contains less than 3,
  the current Assassin pays the shortfall from their personal Influence.

Killing a player who does not match the Hit still resolves the assassination, but
the Hit fails. Submitting no valid target also fails the Hit.

## 11.4 Assassin: Assassinate

The Assassin must attempt one assassination per round.

To make a valid assassination:

1. Choose another living player who is not the Accomplice.
2. **Either you or the Accomplice** must have given that player at least 1 Influence
   **at some point during the game**. It does not have to be this round, and it does not
   have to be you — the courier can deliver the bribe.
3. During the Private Phase, privately send or whisper the victim's name to the GM.
4. Do not change the target after the Private Phase ends.

At the beginning of the Council:

1. The GM announces the victim.
2. Half of the victim's Influence, rounded down, enters the Stash.
3. Resolve the rest of Death and Ghosts.
4. The GM announces whether the Hit succeeded.

If the Assassin submits no target, targets themselves or the Accomplice, names an
already dead player, or neither Syndicate member has ever given the target any
Influence, no one is Assassinated and the Hit fails.

## 11.5 Accomplice: Launder

Once per round during the Private Phase, the Accomplice may secretly give the GM up to
2 personal Influence. Add it to the Stash.

Once per round, the current Assassin may withdraw up to 2 Influence from the Stash.

## 11.6 Promotion

If the current Assassin is Executed, the Accomplice immediately becomes the new
Assassin.

The new Assassin:

- gains Assassinate;
- inherits the Stash **in full** and the Syndicate Victory condition; and
- keeps their original Faction, Motive, Ambition, Secret, and Evidence.

When an Assassin is Executed, the GM immediately announces one **true** statement
about the surviving Syndicate member — their Motive, Ambition, Secret, or Starting
Advantage. This statement does not count toward the five Evidence pieces.

The original Assassin becomes a Ghost and now shares the Accomplice's objective: they
win if the new Assassin wins.

Existing Evidence does not change after Promotion.

# 12. GM Reference

## 12.1 Player-count scaling

For an odd number of major-faction seats, randomly decide whether the Aristocrats or
Reformers receive the extra player. Do this before assigning Roles.

| Players | Aristocrats / Reformers | Magnates | Magnate threshold | Assassin + Stash threshold |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 3 / 3 | 2 | 16 | 46 |
| 9 | 4 / 3 | 2 | 16 | 44 |
| 10 | 4 / 4 | 2 | 16 | 42 |
| 11 | 4 / 4 | 3 | 24 | 42 |
| 12 | 5 / 4 | 3 | 24 | 40 |
| 13 | 5 / 5 | 3 | 24 | 40 |
| 14 | 6 / 5 | 3 | 24 | 41 |
| 15 | 6 / 6 | 3 | 24 | 39 |

Simply: **Magnates need 16 if there are two of them, or 24 if there are three. The
Assassin needs roughly 40, a little more at small tables.**

## 12.2 Hit deck

Choose one Hit per round. Do not repeat a Hit in the same game. Only use a
Hit if at least two valid assassination targets can currently qualify.

Eligibility is checked at the end of the Private Phase unless the card says otherwise.
Across the three rounds, avoid repeatedly selecting Hits that concentrate pressure
on the same Faction.

| Hit | Valid victim |
|---|---|
| **High Society** | A player in the current Top 3 by Influence, as privately verified by the GM |
| **Golden Goose** | A player holding at least 10 Influence |
| **Break the Old Guard** | An Aristocrat |
| **Break the New Order** | A Reformer |
| **Cut the Purse Strings** | A Magnate |
| **Burn the Evidence** | A player who received Evidence, including Auction Evidence |
| **Silence the Accused** | A player who has previously been the target of an Interrogation |
| **Untouchable** | A player who has never been Interrogated and whose Secret has never been correctly Exposed |

For Round 1, remove any Hit that cannot yet have a valid target.

## 12.3 Evidence setup

1. Generate one truthful statement for each of the five Evidence templates.
2. Reserve one of those truthful statements for the Auction.
3. Select two of the four remaining statements and replace each with a statement that
   is definitely false under its template.
4. Distribute those four statements as Starting Advantages.
5. Give every remaining player truthful Secret Intelligence as their Starting
   Advantage.

For templates 1, 2, 3, and 5, do not accidentally make a false statement true for the
other Syndicate member.

## 12.4 Setup checklist

- [ ] Select the scaling row for the player count.
- [ ] Randomize the larger major Faction if needed.
- [ ] Assign exactly one Aristocrat and one Reformer to the Syndicate.
- [ ] Assign the Assassin and Accomplice between those two players.
- [ ] Prepare four Starting Evidence statements and one Auction statement.
- [ ] Verify that exactly two Evidence statements are false.
- [ ] Assign each player a Motive, Ambition, Secret, outside-Faction name, and Starting
      Advantage.
- [ ] Deal **Collector to at most one player** and **Vanity to at most three**, since
      only one player can win the Auction and only three can be on the Leaderboard.
- [ ] Give every player 4 Influence.
- [ ] Tell the Assassin and Accomplice each other's identities.
- [ ] Announce Faction counts, Syndicate placement, and the Evidence noise budget.
- [ ] Prepare three eligible Hits.
- [ ] Track the Interrogation cost, Stash, debts, Goals, and private Influence totals.
- [ ] At the start of the Final, fix who is Bankrupt once.
